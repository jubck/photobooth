i love vintage photo booths but theyre so expensive due to film materials becoming super expensive over the past two decades. for my birthday, I wanted to provide my guests with something fun that I could just have right on my ipad and print instantly! 

note: these photo strips are expected to be printed on a 4x3 postcard sized paper. if you are forking the repo for your own personal use, be sure to change some of the personal changes I made to the app mentioning the selphy cp1300 (which is my preferred printer for this) and the default message about my birthday.
